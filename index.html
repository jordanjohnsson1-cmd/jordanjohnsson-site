About Me

I'm a junior at Palo Alto High School and a sprinter for Paly and the East Palo Alto Greyhounds. I hold my school's all-time 100-meter record. Off the track I play varsity soccer, teach piano to high school and elementary students, speak German at home, and build things as a hobbyist tinkerer: code and hardware. I like competing, and I like building things until they run on their own.

Greene Scholars science fairs

2026: 1st Place

"Botanic Glow"

1st place, GSP Science Fair. Evaluated the chemical efficacy of commercial hair treatments on follicle integrity and moisture retention, using quantitative analysis to identify the most effective formulations.

2025: 2nd Place

Hair products comparison study

Compared natural hair products, including argan and castor oil, on hair health and moisture retention to determine which ingredients performed best.

2024: 2nd Place

"Juggernaut"

Applied the physics of center of gravity to build a solution for juggling a weighted soccer ball.

2023: 1st Place

"Flower Power"

1st place, GSP Science Fair. Co-designed a model car powered by a wind turbine to run lights and a fan, showing how small sustainable modifications can cut vehicle energy use.

2022: 3rd Place

"Cube Lube"

Tested which lubricants most effectively speed up Rubik's Cube solves: a first hands-on lesson in viscosity.
